# Privacy Policy for Testandi

**Last Updated:** September 22, 2026

Testandi ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Testandi mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect

Testandi is designed with a privacy-first approach. 
*   **Local Data Storage:** The calculations, inputs, and results you generate using the App are stored entirely locally on your device utilizing built-in state management (Zustand). 
*   **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or financial account details.
*   **No External Servers:** Your financial inputs and calculated data are never transmitted to, or stored on, our servers.

## 2. Usage Data and Analytics

Because the App is built using the Expo framework and deployed on iOS and Android platforms, standard, anonymized crash reports and app usage telemetry may be collected by Apple, Google, or Expo. This data does not contain your personal financial inputs and is used strictly to improve app stability and performance.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Testandi does not use third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that track your cross-app behavior. 

## 4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. Since all calculation data is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your device's native security features (e.g., passcode, biometric locks). We recommend securing your device to protect your saved calculations.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
